RHD - LifeCore | Steam Workshop Required Items
================================================

When publishing RHD - LifeCore to Steam Workshop, mark these Workshop items
as REQUIRED DEPENDENCIES in the Steam Workshop uploader/website:

1. CBA_A3
   Workshop ID: 450814997
   Required by: RHD LifeCore addon configuration

2. cTab+
   Workshop ID: 2262006564
   Required by: RHD LifeCore tablet/UI integration

3. Antistasi Ultimate
   Workshop ID: 3020755032
   Required by: the published RHD Altis scenario and A3A bridge

ACE3 is NOT required by RHD. It is an optional runtime enhancement.

IMPORTANT
---------
Steam Workshop dependency relationships are publisher metadata. They are not
created merely by adding a text file to the mod. The RHD addon itself also
hard-declares cba_main, ctab_core and A3A_core in CfgPatches so Arma 3 will
reject an incomplete required-addon loadout.

Recommended published load order:
CBA_A3 -> cTab+ -> Antistasi Ultimate -> RHD - LifeCore
